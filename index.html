What the application does with Google

One Off Desktop requests two permissions, and only these two:

  • youtube.upload — to upload a finished video file to the channel.
  • youtube.force-ssl — to set the custom thumbnail on that video.

It does not read your email, your Drive, your contacts or your watch history. It cannot delete the channel. It runs on the owner's own computer; there is no server, no database of users, and no third party receiving data. The full detail is in the privacy policy.

Honesty about money

One Off is made from Iran, which is not currently a country where YouTube's Partner Programme is available. There is no advertising revenue here and the channel does not pretend otherwise. The point is the craft and the watch time, not the payout.

Where to find it

The channel is on YouTube as @OneOffTech. The engine that produces the episodes is built in the open.
